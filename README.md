## The entry

> My smart fridge emailed me a diet plan, padlocked the ice cream, and CC'd my doctor — I just wanted a midnight snack.

The joke is a smart appliance that has taken its job far too seriously: the fridge doesn't just keep food cold, it acts like a concerned parent — emailing a diet plan, padlocking the ice cream, and escalating the whole thing to the doctor. The punchline ("I just wanted a midnight snack") shrinks the drama back down to one small, relatable craving, which is what makes the fridge's overreaction funny. Every word earns its place — three escalating actions, then one deflating confession.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single line (119 characters, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`. It is deterministic: no network, no randomness, no files read from outside the repository. Before writing, it checks the line against the competition's gate (1–200 characters, not only whitespace) and refuses to write an invalid line.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
